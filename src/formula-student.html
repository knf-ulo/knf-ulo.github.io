What is Formula Student?

Formula Student is the world's largest engineering competition, organized by SAE International and IMechE. Teams design and build single-seat race cars, competing in dynamic events on track (acceleration, autocross, endurance) and in design reviews and scrutineering before industry engineers from across the motorsport world.

The competition does not pick the fastest car. It picks the best-engineered one. Teams showcase engineering excellence, business acumen and driving skills on circuits across the globe, with static events (Design, Cost Report, Business Plan) carrying equal weight alongside performance on track.

Who are we?

KNF is a high school physics club at Uniwersyteckie Liceum Ogólnokształcące im. Pawła Adamowicza in Gdańsk (ULO): a team of high school students dedicated to pushing the boundaries of what a school project can achieve. We started with a question: what if we built a car from scratch, grounding every decision in physics and data? Today we have five active car subsystems, a growing team, and a precise development plan.

The road to 2027

What we've done, where we are now, and what's left before our first competition.

  1. Phase one

    Research and design

    Car architecture, CFD and FEA, suspension geometry and the requirements for every subsystem. No part is ordered without the numbers behind it.

  2. 2025

    The club starts

    A group of ULO students sets out to build a Formula Student car from scratch, starting with the rulebook and the physics.

  3. 2026

    Front wing in CFD

    The first front wing geometry simulated in CFD. The flow and pressure results now steer the design.

  4. 2026

    Frame concept and first FEA

    A chromoly (Cr-Mo) spaceframe layout, loaded in finite element analysis (FEA) to see where it bends.

  5. 2026

    First full car render

    Every subsystem modelled together in one car for the first time.

  6. Now

    We are here

    Each area works on its own, and every month we check how it all fits together.

    • AerodynamicsIn progress

      Front wing CFD simulations in progress. Base geometry defined, optimising angle of attack and section profile.

    • ChassisIn progress

      Preliminary Cr-Mo space frame layout complete. FEA underway for torsional stiffness and side-impact load cases under the Formula Student Germany (FSG) rulebook.

    • SuspensionIn progress

      Double-wishbone geometry defined. Work is underway on the suspension mounting points (hardpoints) and the roll centre for the target car mass.

    • TelemetryPlanning

      Sensor list defined. Picking the microcontroller and laying out the CAN bus, the car's data network. Goal: our own telemetry board.

    • PowertrainPlanning

      Comparing engine candidates. Criteria: mass, reliability, parts availability and the rulebook (the 20 mm air-intake restrictor).

  7. Phase two

    Manufacturing and integration

    We make the parts, weld the frame and bring the subsystems together. Every joint between assemblies is checked before the car is put together.

  8. Next

    First start-up in the workshop

    The engine fires in the assembled car for the first time.

  9. Phase three

    Testing and preparation

    Track testing, suspension and setup tuning, and the Design Event, Cost Report and Business Plan documents.

  10. 2027

    Our first competition

    The goal: racing in the combustion class at Formula Student Germany.
