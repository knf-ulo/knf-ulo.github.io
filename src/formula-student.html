Introduction

What is Formula Student?

Formula Student is the world's largest engineering competition for university students, organized by SAE International and IMechE. Teams design and build single-seat race cars, competing in dynamic events on track — acceleration, autocross, endurance — and in design reviews and scrutineering before industry engineers from across the motorsport world.

The competition does not pick the fastest car — it picks the best-engineered one. Teams showcase engineering excellence, business acumen and driving skills on circuits across the globe, with static events — Design, Cost Report, Business Plan — carrying equal weight alongside performance on track.

Team

Who are we?

KNF is a student research club based in the physics faculty — a team of engineering students dedicated to pushing the boundaries of what a university project can achieve. We started with a question: what if we built a car from scratch, grounding every decision in physics and data? Today we have five active subsystems, a growing team, and a precise development plan.

Timeline

Our plan

Building the car is a multi-stage engineering project split into three key phases. Each phase has concrete checkpoints — we iterate, we don't guess.

Phase 01

Research & Design

We define the car architecture, run CFD and FEA analyses, design suspension geometry, select the drivetrain, and set requirements for each subsystem. No part is ordered without calculation backing.

Phase 02

Manufacturing & Integration

We manufacture components, weld the frame, integrate subsystems. Every interface between assemblies is verified before final assembly. Goal: first engine-start in the workshop.

Phase 03

Testing & Preparation

Dynamic testing on track, suspension calibration, setup optimisation. Preparation of complete Design Event, Cost Report and Business Plan documentation matching Formula Student requirements.
