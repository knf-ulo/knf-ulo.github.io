Projects

Our active engineering areas — from car subsystems to independent research.

Photo Gallery Our FS Project
  1. 01 Aerodynamics We design the Formula Student aero package — front wing, rear diffuser and bodywork — using CFD simulation. We iterate aerofoil geometry, analyse downforce and drag across configurations, and every change is verified quantitatively before moving to the next revision.
  2. 02 Chassis & Powertrain A Cr-Mo space frame designed with FEA for torsional stiffness and side-impact resistance per the FSG rulebook. In parallel we select the engine, gearbox and cooling system — every decision weighted by mass, reliability and regulatory compliance.
  3. 03 Software We build our own engineering tools — scripts for analysing telemetry data, a live sensor visualisation dashboard, and scripts that automate repetitive design calculations. The goal is to tighten the iteration loop: a measurement result should feed the next design decision in minutes, not days.
  4. 04 Materials Research We run our own material and sensor tests independent of the current car build. We test fatigue properties of welded joints, calibrate force and deflection sensors, and verify supplier datasheet claims on our own rigs. The results feed a knowledge base we will draw on for future car generations.

The Team

We are engineering students passionate about pushing the boundaries of what a university team can build — designing our first car from scratch, and laying the groundwork for the next generation of motorsport engineers.

01 Integrity

Every design decision is grounded in data. We document assumptions, verify calculations, and share results — even when they show a mistake. Integrity means treating our own shortcomings as material to work with, not reasons for embarrassment.

02 Reliability

A car that doesn't finish the run isn't fast. Every subsystem is designed for repeatability, ease of service, and resilience under competition conditions. Our target is zero mechanical failures — not as a slogan, but as a measurable engineering goal.

03 Precision

A tolerance is not a formality — it is the difference between a design that works and one that fails. From CAD to the test bench, we track every number. Results are compared against simulations and fed into the next iteration.

04 Teamwork

Aerodynamics does not exist without the chassis. Powertrain without telemetry is blind. We work as one system — with regular integration reviews, clear ownership, and a culture where problems are raised early, not buried.

05 Ambition

Our first car doesn't exist yet, but the schedule, calculations, and design decisions do. We're building not just a car, but the foundation of a team that will be better than ourselves.
